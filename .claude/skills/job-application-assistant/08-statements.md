# Statements

Your research and teaching statements are **your** documents. They live in
`documents/statements/`, you edit them there, and this file holds only what the
workspace needs to reason about them: an assessment, the gaps, and where the source
is. There is no second copy of the prose - two copies drift, and the copy the
committee reads is the one on disk.

| Statement | Source file | Last read | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | `documents/statements/research_statement.tex` | [DATE] | [yes/no] |
| Teaching | `documents/statements/teaching_statement.tex` | [DATE] | [yes/no] |

`.tex`, `.md`, `.txt` and `.pdf` are all readable. `/apply` tailors a copy of the
source per posting; it never edits the source.

profile and the statement name different papers, ask - do not choose.

## Other statements

Diversity, service, mentoring and any statement a posting names beyond research and
teaching are **not** collected here. `/apply` handles them per posting: it lists the
posting's required documents verbatim, says which exist, and for the rest asks you
for a draft or offers to build one from this profile alone. A statement written for
one posting stays in that packet.
